## Overview
Raymond's self-assessed working style centers on high autonomy, comfort with ambiguity, and situational adaptability. He calibrates decision speed, collaboration mode, and communication style to what the moment actually requires rather than defaulting to one fixed gear.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy | High | Thrives with latitude to define and drive the approach, not just execute a brief |
| Ambiguity Tolerance | High | Energized by fast-paced, undefined problem spaces over stable, well-scoped work |
| Collaboration Style | Adaptive | Shifts between solo execution and tight cross-functional pairing depending on what the project needs |
| Risk Calibration | Contextual | Fast and decisive on low-stakes, reversible calls; deliberate and data-first on high-stakes, hard-to-reverse ones |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Proactive ownership without a brief:** repeatedly identified and fixed systems nobody had flagged (Medallia survey triggers, returns web forms, EHC IVR) and expanded scope past what was assigned (AI IVR global rollout, Custom Copilot adoption tool).
- **Constructive pushback:** challenges unfounded targets or asks (Metro Mobile license growth) but always pairs the pushback with a credible, evidence-based alternative rather than a flat no.
- **Cross-functional bridge-building:** sustained multi-year alignment work across 8 product teams and engineering; mentors other PMs without formal authority over them.

## How You Work Best
- Collaborative, cross-team environments with real autonomy over approach
- Fast-paced, ambiguous problem spaces over stable, fully-scoped work
- Environments that let decision speed match actual stakes, rather than mandating one universal cadence (heavy process for everything, or move-fast-and-break-things for everything)

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "high autonomy," "ambiguous," "fast-paced," "ownership," "0-to-1," "cross-functional"
- "bias for action" paired with "data-driven" — language close to Amazon's one-way-door/two-way-door framing maps directly onto Raymond's stakes-based decision calibration; worth naming explicitly if a posting uses that framing

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- Heavily process-driven cultures that mandate the same approval/decision cadence regardless of stakes
- Roles scoped as narrow, repetitive execution with little latitude to define the "how"

## Management Style Preferences
- **What works:** a clear outcome with latitude on approach; stakeholder relationships where pushback is expected and welcomed, not penalized
- **What doesn't:** being locked into a single decision-making speed or process regardless of the actual risk of the call

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** lead with the "matches decision speed to stakes" framing when a posting signals either extreme (all-process or all-speed) — it differentiates from candidates who only have one gear.
- **CV:** emphasize the initiative-without-a-brief pattern (Medallia/web forms, AI IVR, Custom Copilot) since it's the most evidenced trait across multiple stories.
- **Interviews:** Metro Mobile License Growth for "decision-making style" / "tell me about a time you pushed back"; Copilot Pilot for "high-stakes, data-first" decisions; Medallia/AI IVR for "fast, autonomous, ambiguous" examples.
- **Don't overstate:** no formal assessment is on file. This profile is self-reported and inferred from work history, not a validated instrument — say so plainly if an employer asks directly.
